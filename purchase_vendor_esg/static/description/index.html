The Compliance Blindspot

Purchasing teams often unknowingly issue high-value purchase orders to suppliers with expired environmental certifications (ISO 14001), poor human rights records, or high carbon emission footprints — exposing enterprises to massive regulatory penalties and reputational damage.

The XodetiStudio Solution

An automated ESG evaluation engine embedded directly inside Odoo 18.0 Purchasing. Automatically calculates vendor scores (0-100 pts), tracks certificate expiration dates, rewards local suppliers to reduce carbon emissions, and proactively intercepts unapproved Purchase Orders.

FEATURE 02 · ACTIVE GUARDRAIL

Smart PO Intercept & Confirmation Blocking

When a buyer attempts to confirm a Purchase Order for a non-compliant ("Red") supplier, the system proactively blocks the action and outputs an informative explanation detailing missing mandatory certifications.

  • Zero Compliance Bypass: Prevents rogue buyers from committing company funds to non-certified vendors.
  • Clear Remediation Instructions: Tells buyers exactly which certificate is expired or missing.
FEATURE 03 · OPERATIONAL FLEXIBILITY

Manager Override with Immutable Audit Logging

Need to purchase critical components from a non-compliant vendor in an operational emergency? Authorized purchasing managers can toggle the Force / Override Compliance switch.

  • Persistent Visual Warning: Adds a noticeable warning ribbon to the PO.
  • Audit Trail: Logs the approving manager, timestamp, and justification into the chatter.
